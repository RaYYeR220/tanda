Three Pillars of the New Tanda

  • I.
    AI Reliability Scoring
    An AI underwriter reads each member's on-chain history and sets their collateral requirement. High-trust members stake less. New wallets stake more. No bias, no favoritism.
  • II.
    Default-Proof by Design
    If a member defaults, their staked collateral is slashed and our insurance pool covers any gap. The recipient is paid in full — every time. No organizer holds the money.
  • III.
    Gasless Onboarding
    Join with a passkey. No seed phrase. No gas fees. No crypto literacy required. Built for the next wave of LATAM users — the unbanked majority.

AI-organized savings circles on-chain. Save together, in pesos, without the trust problem. Your circle, your rules — secured by a smart contract on Arbitrum, settled in MXNB.
