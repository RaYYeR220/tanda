Características principales
  • I.
    AI Reliability Scoring

    An on-chain AI underwriter reads each member's transaction history and assigns a reliability score. Collateral is set to match — high trust earns lower stakes.

  • II.
    Default-Proof by Design

    If a member defaults, their collateral is slashed and an insurance pool tops up the shortfall. The recipient is always paid in full. Always.

  • III.
    Gasless Onboarding

    Join with a passkey. No seed phrase, no gas fees, no crypto expertise required. Built for the next wave of LATAM users.
