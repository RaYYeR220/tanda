"La tanda que siempre conociste — sin el riesgo de que te dejen colgado."

AI-organized savings circles on-chain. Save together, in pesos, without the trust problem. Your money is protected by smart contracts and an AI underwriter — not by handshakes.

🤖
Puntaje IA de confianza
An AI underwriter reads your on-chain history and sets the collateral you must stake — no bank needed. Low-risk members put up less; high-risk members put up more.
🔒
A prueba de impagos
If someone defaults, their staked collateral is slashed and an insurance pool covers any shortfall. The recipient always gets paid in full. Always.
📲
Onboarding sin fricción
Join with a passkey — no seed phrase, no gas, no exchange account. Built for the next wave of LATAM users, including the unbanked.
